# Research note: what Israeli construction and real estate media publish (Oct 2026)

WebFetch was blocked; findings are from search results.

## Post types that get attention
1. **Monthly data "explained"**: every CBS release (building input index on the 15th, home price index, starts and completions, unsold stock) gets a same day story with one headline number and "what it means for buyers". Sites: klikatnadlan.co.il, nadlancenter.co.il, Calcalist, Bizportal, Funder, JDN.
2. **Interest rate and mortgage stories**: each Bank of Israel decision plus the monthly mortgage volume ("record since December 2024"), and the 80/20 contractor-loan crackdown (ice.co.il, nadlancenter, Calcalist).
3. **"The complete guide for 2026" evergreen pages**: purchase tax brackets, betterment levy, Sale Law guarantees, private house (nadlancenter 2026 guide series).
4. **Local planning drama**: big plans in Hod HaSharon (HR/1202 re-deposited with 5,210 units, Nof Yarkon 9,000 units postponed again), landowner petitions, district committee decisions (nadlancenter, Globes, Calcalist).
5. **Transport as a value story**: Metro M1 eastern branch through Hod HaSharon, Route 531 and the Sharon railway, new Sharon roads (hamichlol, Maariv, ice).
6. **Regulation that changes the house**: mandatory 5 kW solar on new detached homes with roof over 100 m², mamad permit exemption and 9 to 15 m² enlargement (Calcalist, ice, Bizportal).
7. **Labour and cost pressure**: shortage of about 38k construction workers (Finance Ministry chief economist), Builders Association demands on foreign workers.
8. **Seasonal maintenance**: roof waterproofing before winter, 72 hour flood test (pro.co.il, Midrag), IMS El Nino report (July 2026).
9. Builder brand pages (Tidhar, Electra, Yanko Weiss etc.) were not reachable for content analysis; trade media shows they appear mostly through sales records and project launches.

## What we take for our feed
Data translated to "what it means for a private house", local planning status, checklists, myths, money explainers, polls, seasonal.
